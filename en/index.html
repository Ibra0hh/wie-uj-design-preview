About Us

IEEE stands for the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers, a global professional organization that advances technology through research, education, collaboration, and technical standards.

Women in Engineering (WIE) is an IEEE community dedicated to supporting women in engineering and inspiring girls to pursue science and technology.

IEEE WIE UJ is a student-led community at the University of Jordan dedicated to promoting STEM, engineering, and innovation.

Through educational initiatives, hands-on experiences, workshops, and community engagement, we aim to develop technical skills, inspire curiosity, and connect students with real-world opportunities while encouraging the next generation to explore science, technology, and engineering.
